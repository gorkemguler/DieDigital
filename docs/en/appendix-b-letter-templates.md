Copy, fill in the [brackets], send. Delete the lines you don't need. Keep formal letters polite but firm; keep personal requests short and friendly.

## 1. GDPR erasure request

**Subject:** Erasure request under Article 17 GDPR

> Dear Data Protection Officer / Privacy Team,
>
> I am writing to exercise my rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") [and the UK GDPR] regarding the personal data that [Company] processes about me.
>
> **Data subject**
> Name: [Full name]
> Contact email: [email]
> Postal address: [address, only if needed]
>
> **Identifiers you may hold about me**
> - Account email: [email]
> - Username / profile URL: [username / link]
> - Phone: [number]
>
> **My requests**
> 1. Erase all personal data concerning me without undue delay (Article 17(1)) and permanently close any account associated with the identifiers above.
> 2. Notify every recipient to whom my personal data has been disclosed of this erasure (Article 19). Where you have made my data public, take reasonable steps to inform other controllers that are processing it (Article 17(2)).
> 3. Stop processing my personal data for direct marketing, profiling and advertising (Article 21(2) and 21(3)), effective immediately.
> 4. If you believe any data must be retained to comply with a legal obligation, tell me which data, on what legal basis and for how long, and erase it as soon as that period ends.
> 5. Before erasure, tell me which personal data you hold about me, where you obtained it and to whom you have disclosed it (Article 15(1)(c) and (g)).
>
> Under Article 12(3) you must respond within one month of receiving this request. Please confirm in writing once the erasure is complete. If I do not receive a satisfactory response, I will lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority (Article 77).
>
> This request is sent from the email address registered with my account. Please do not ask for more personal data than is strictly necessary to verify my identity (Article 12(6)).
>
> Kind regards,
> [Date]
> [Full name]

*Note:* Drop request 5 if you already know what they hold (for example, your own account).

## 2. GDPR reminder

**Subject:** Follow-up: unanswered GDPR erasure request [reference number, if any]

> Dear Data Protection Officer / Privacy Team,
>
> On [date] I asked [Company] to erase all personal data concerning me under Article 17 GDPR. The one-month deadline set by Article 12(3) has passed and I have not received a satisfactory answer.
>
> I repeat my original requests: erase all of my personal data, notify any recipients of the erasure (Article 19), and confirm completion in writing.
>
> If I do not hear from you within 14 days, I will lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority under Article 77 GDPR without further notice.
>
> Name: [Full name] · Email: [email] · Account identifiers: [email, username]
>
> Kind regards,
> [Date]
> [Full name]

## 3. Complaint to a data protection authority

Most authorities have an online complaint form; adapt this for the free-text field or a letter.

> **Complainant:** [Full name, address, email, phone]
> **Controller complained about:** [Company name, address, website, privacy contact]
>
> **Subject:** Failure to comply with my erasure request under Article 17 GDPR.
>
> **Facts:**
> 1. On [date] I sent [Company] a request to erase my personal data under Article 17 GDPR [from the email address registered with my account / through their privacy form] (Annex 1).
> 2. On [date] I sent a reminder (Annex 2).
> 3. [To date I have received no reply. / In its reply of [date], the company refused my request on the following grounds: ... (Annex 3). These grounds are not valid because ...]
>
> **Request:** I ask the authority to investigate this matter and to order the controller to erase my personal data and to respond properly to my request.
>
> **Annexes:** 1. Request and proof of sending · 2. Reminder · 3. The company's reply, if any
>
> [Date] · [Full name]

In the UK the authority is the ICO (https://ico.org.uk). In the EU, the European Data Protection Board's website lists every national authority.

## 4. US state privacy law deletion request (CCPA etc.)

**Subject:** Request to delete personal information and opt out of sale/sharing

> To the Privacy Team of [Company],
>
> I am a resident of [State]. Under the [California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA / applicable state privacy law], I request that you:
>
> 1. Delete all personal information you have collected about me, and direct your service providers and contractors to do the same.
> 2. Stop selling or sharing my personal information, including for cross-context behavioral advertising.
> 3. Tell me the categories and specific pieces of personal information you hold about me, the sources you obtained it from, and the third parties you have disclosed it to.
>
> **Identifiers you may hold about me:** [full name, email addresses, phone numbers, current and past addresses, username / profile URL]
>
> Please confirm when the deletion is complete. Under the law you must respond within 45 days. If you need to verify my identity, please ask only for information necessary for that purpose.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Date]
> [Full name]

*Note:* For data brokers, use their opt-out page first ([chapter 5](05-data-brokers-and-lookup-apps.md)); send this if there isn't one or it doesn't work. California residents can also use DROP.

## 5. Search engine delisting request

Use the search engine's own form; adapt this for the explanation field.

> I request that the following URLs no longer be shown in search results for my name ([Full name]):
>
> - [URL 1]
> - [URL 2]
>
> **Reasons:**
> - The information on these pages is [more than X years old / out of date / inaccurate / taken out of context].
> - I have no public role, and the information has no current news value or public interest.
> - Showing these results alongside my name affects my private life and the protection of my personal data, and is causing me concrete harm [at work / in my social life / to my safety].
> - I make this request under Article 17 GDPR [/ the applicable data protection law].
>
> If my request is refused or not answered, I reserve the right to complain to the data protection authority.

## 6. Wayback Machine removal

**To:** info@archive.org · **Subject:** Request to remove archived pages containing my personal data

> Hello,
>
> I am asking you to remove the archived captures of the following URLs from the Wayback Machine and to exclude them from future crawls. These pages contain my personal information (name, contact details and/or photographs), and their continued availability affects my privacy.
>
> - [URL 1]
> - [URL 2]
>
> [I am the owner of this site and can verify it on request. / I am the person named on these pages and request removal under Article 17 GDPR.]
>
> Thank you for your help.
> [Full name] · [email]

## 7. Removal request to a site administrator

**Subject:** Removing my personal data from your website

> Hello,
>
> The following pages on your site contain my name and personal details:
>
> - [URL] — [what's there: my name, my photo, my phone number, something I wrote]
>
> I'd like to ask you to remove this information from the page. If removing it completely isn't possible, reducing my name to initials and removing my photo would also be fine.
>
> I'm making this request under [Article 17 GDPR / the applicable data protection law]; I'd appreciate hearing back within [30] days.
>
> Thank you,
> [Full name / username]

## 8. Anonymisation request to a news site

**Subject:** Request to anonymise my name in "[Article title]" ([date])

> Dear Editor,
>
> Your article "[article title]" published on [date] ([URL]) names me and shows [my photo / my age / my address].
>
> [X] years have passed since publication; the matter [has been resolved / is no longer current], and I have no public role. Having the article available under my name today causes disproportionate harm to my private life and [career / social life].
>
> I ask you to remove the article or, if you wish to preserve its news value, to replace my name with initials ([A.B.]) and remove or blur my photo. If my name appears in the page title or URL, I also ask for those to be updated.
>
> I make this request on the basis of my right to privacy and [Article 17 GDPR / the applicable data protection law].
>
> Yours sincerely,
> [Full name] · [email]

## 9. Message to friends and family

> Hi [name], a small favour: I've been trying to reduce my footprint online for a while. There are a few photos on [Instagram / Facebook] where you've tagged me or I'm in them ([link / which photo]). Could you take them down or crop me out? And in future, could you check with me before posting? Thanks so much, and don't worry, it's nothing to do with you :)

For a group:

> Hi everyone, one request: from now on please don't tag me in photos, and check with me before posting any. And please don't mention me when you share where you are. Thanks!

## 10. To a former employer or school

**Subject:** Removing my details from your website

> Hello,
>
> I [worked at your company / was a student at your school] until [date]. My name and photo still appear in the [team / alumni / achievements / news] section of your website:
>
> - [URL]
>
> I'd like to ask you to remove them. I also ask, under [Article 17 GDPR / the applicable data protection law], that you delete the personal data you hold about me (CV, application documents, contact details), except what you're legally required to keep.
>
> Thank you,
> [Full name] · [email]
