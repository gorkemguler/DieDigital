# 3. Deleting accounts

> Before this chapter, do [2. Content cleanup](02-content-cleanup.md). On some platforms your content stays after the account is deleted, and then you can no longer remove it.

## The same method for every account

1. **Log in** and make sure two-factor authentication works.
2. **Get your money out:** cancel subscriptions (ones bought through an app store must be cancelled in the store), use up balances and gift cards, remove saved cards.
3. **Empty the profile:** name, photo, bio, phone, date of birth, address. If the deletion somehow fails, at least an empty shell is left behind.
4. **Download a backup if you need one** ([chapter 1](01-inventory-and-backup.md)).
5. **Start the deletion.** "Deactivate" and "delete" are different: a deactivated account isn't deleted, just hidden. Keep the confirmation email and a screenshot.
6. **Write it down:** the request date and the date the waiting period ends.
7. **Don't log in during the waiting period.** Remove the app from your phone, log out in the browser, delete that site's cookies, and turn off your password manager's auto-login for it. On most platforms a single login cancels the deletion.
8. **Confirm:** When the period is over, try "forgot password". "No account found" means it's gone. Open the profile URL in a private window; you should get "page not found".

## If you can't find a delete option

- **Check the app.** Apple (since 2022) and Google Play (since 2024) require apps that let you create an account to let you delete it from inside the app. An option you can't find on the website is often in the app under Profile / Account / Privacy / Settings: "delete account", "close account".
- **Search the help centre** for "delete account" or "close account".
- **Support form or email.** "Please delete my account and all personal data you hold about me" is enough.
- **The legal route.** If you get no answer or they say they can't, send a formal data deletion request ([chapter 8](08-legal-rights.md), texts in [Appendix B](appendix-b-letter-templates.md)).
- **JustDeleteMe** (https://justdeleteme.xyz): a community directory of deletion pages for hundreds of services, with difficulty ratings.

> **With the tool:** `diedigital catalog <service>` shows the deletion link, waiting period and pitfalls for known services; `diedigital letters send` prepares and sends the deletion request.

## Shopping, food, classifieds and local services

Online stores, delivery apps, grocery apps, classifieds and second-hand marketplaces:

1. Look for "delete account / close account" in the app's account settings.
2. On classifieds sites, remove your **listings** first: old listings still show your phone number and photos of your home and car.
3. Delete saved addresses and cards.
4. If there's no option, or you want to be sure your data is really deleted, send a deletion request to the privacy contact in the site's privacy policy ([chapter 8](08-legal-rights.md)).

Invoices and order records are kept for a period because of tax law. That's normal; ask in your request to be told which data is kept and for how long.

## Marketing opt-outs

Do this before deleting accounts: some companies keep you on marketing lists even after the account is gone.

- **US:** the National Do Not Call Registry (https://www.donotcall.gov) for telemarketing calls; https://www.optoutprescreen.com for pre-screened credit and insurance offers; DMAchoice for postal marketing.
- **UK:** Telephone Preference Service (TPS) and Mailing Preference Service (MPS).
- **Turkey:** see below (İYS).
- **Everywhere:** unsubscribe from every newsletter, and include "stop all direct marketing" in your data deletion requests.

## Banks, telecoms, government

- **Banks and payment providers:** The account isn't deleted while you're a customer. Close the accounts and cards you don't use; the records are kept for the legal retention period.
- **Mobile operators:** After you cancel, subscriber records are kept for the legal period. Turn off promotional consents.
- **Government portals:** These can't be deleted, and don't need to be. But change the password and turn on two-factor authentication: it's one of the best protections against leaked personal data being misused.

- **E-commerce, delivery and classifieds** (Trendyol, Hepsiburada, n11, Amazon.com.tr, Yemeksepeti, Getir, sahibinden.com, Dolap, letgo and others): same method as above. If there's no delete option, send a KVKK request to the address in the site's *Aydınlatma Metni*; if you can't find it, search the company on VERBİS ([chapter 8](08-legal-rights.md)).
- **İYS (İleti Yönetim Sistemi):** All the SMS, call and email consents you've given to brands are held centrally. Through iys.org.tr or e-Devlet you can see and withdraw all of them in one place.
- **e-Devlet:** Change your password and turn on two-factor authentication.

## Category by category

- **Dating apps:** Deleting the app doesn't delete the account. Cancel the subscription in the App Store / Google Play, then delete the account inside the app. Screenshots of your photos may remain with other people.
- **Gaming accounts:** Steam, Epic, PlayStation, Xbox, Riot, Battle.net. Your purchases go with the account; your username may remain in leaderboards and match histories.
- **Job sites:** LinkedIn, Indeed and local job boards, companies' careers portals, freelancing platforms. Delete your CV first, then the account. The companies you applied to have your CV in their HR systems too; send them a deletion request.
- **School and course platforms:** Alumni portals, learning platforms, online courses. Certificate verification pages are indexed under your name.
- **Travel:** Airline loyalty programmes, hotel chains, booking sites, car rental. Your passport details may be stored.
- **Health and fitness:** Appointment apps, gym memberships, period and pregnancy trackers, nutrition and sleep apps. Health data is a special category of personal data, which makes the deletion request even more important.
- **AI chat assistants:** Delete your chat history ("delete all chats" in settings), turn off use of your data for model training, then close the account.
- **Smart home and car apps:** [chapter 7](07-real-world-traces.md).
- **News site accounts and Disqus:** Comments first, then the account.
- **Petition sites:** Remove your name from petitions you signed, then delete the account.

## Orphaned accounts

Accounts you've lost access to (forgotten password, closed email, changed number):

1. Try the account recovery options (old email, identity verification, recovery codes).
2. Use the platform's "I can't access my account" support flow, prove who you are and ask for deletion.
3. If that fails, send a formal data deletion request including everything that shows the account is yours (username, the year you opened it, old email, profile URL).
