# Appendix A. Checklist

The whole guide as a checklist. Copy it (to an offline document or paper) and work through it in order. Numbers in brackets point to the relevant chapter. If you're aiming for level 1 (invisible), the ★ items are enough; for levels 2 and 3, do all of them.

## Before you start (0)

- [ ] ★ I've chosen my target level (invisible / accountless / clean break)
- [ ] ★ I've thought about who I'm hiding from
- [ ] ★ If I'm hiding from a specific person, I've done chapter 12 first
- [ ] I've decided who I don't want to lose touch with and given them another way to reach me

## Inventory and backup (1)

- [ ] ★ I've stopped posting and opening new accounts
- [ ] ★ Location services, location history and photo location tagging are off
- [ ] ★ I have a list of all my email addresses, phone numbers and usernames
- [ ] I've tried to recover old email addresses I lost access to
- [ ] ★ Password manager and browser entries are in the inventory
- [ ] Every mailbox searched for "welcome / verify / receipt / unsubscribe"
- [ ] "Sign in with Google / Apple / Facebook" connections listed
- [ ] All my addresses and numbers checked on Have I Been Pwned
- [ ] My usernames searched on search engines and the big sites
- [ ] App store history, subscriptions and card statements reviewed
- [ ] Memory checklist worked through category by category
- [ ] ★ Data exports requested, downloaded and stored encrypted, offline, in two copies

## Content cleanup (2)

- [ ] ★ Accounts I'm keeping are private / only me
- [ ] ★ Photos of my face, posts with locations, and anything mentioning my phone, address, school or work deleted
- [ ] ★ Facebook activity log cleaned up (posts, comments, tags, groups)
- [ ] ★ Instagram posts, story archive, comments and tags cleaned up
- [ ] X posts protected; sensitive ones deleted
- [ ] TikTok and YouTube videos, and my comments on other videos, deleted
- [ ] ★ Google Maps reviews and photos deleted
- [ ] LinkedIn activity and profile details cleaned up
- [ ] Reddit comments and posts overwritten and deleted
- [ ] Discord messages deleted / deletion requested
- [ ] Deletable Telegram and WhatsApp messages deleted; personal details removed from groups before leaving
- [ ] GitHub repositories, gists and profile details cleaned up
- [ ] My posts on forums and review sites deleted
- [ ] Public profiles like Spotify, Strava, Swarm and Goodreads cleaned up
- [ ] ★ Contact syncing off; uploaded address books deleted
- [ ] My own site/blog removed with noindex → 410
- [ ] ★ Checked from a private window

## Deleting accounts (3)

- [ ] For every account: subscriptions cancelled, balance used, cards removed, profile emptied
- [ ] Deletions started; confirmations kept; waiting period end dates in the list
- [ ] Apps removed and sessions logged out so I don't log in during waiting periods
- [ ] Shopping, delivery and classifieds sites: listings, addresses and cards deleted, accounts closed
- [ ] Marketing opt-outs done (Do Not Call, TPS/MPS, İYS or local equivalent)
- [ ] Unused bank accounts, cards and payment apps closed
- [ ] ★ Government portal passwords changed; two-factor authentication on
- [ ] Dating, gaming, job, school, travel, health and AI accounts closed
- [ ] Recovery or legal requests made for accounts I lost access to
- [ ] Accounts past their waiting period confirmed deleted

## Search engines and archives (4)

- [ ] ★ Searches for name, name+city, name+work/school, spelling variants, email, username, phone, address and plate run on four search engines
- [ ] ★ Reverse image searches done
- [ ] ★ Source removed or removal requested for every result
- [ ] ★ Google "outdated content" and Bing content removal requests made for removed sources
- [ ] Google "Results about you" and personal information removal forms used
- [ ] Right to be forgotten / legal route started for sources that couldn't be removed
- [ ] Removal requested for Wayback Machine captures
- [ ] ★ Google Alerts set up

## Data brokers and lookup apps (5)

- [ ] ★ Unlisted from Truecaller; deletion request sent to Getcontact
- [ ] WhatsApp, Telegram and Signal privacy settings locked down
- [ ] (If relevant) removal requested from people-search sites; DROP used if in California
- [ ] Opt-outs sent to face search engines
- [ ] Advertising IDs and personalised ads turned off

## Traces held by others (6)

- [ ] ★ Friends and family asked to remove photos and tags; rules for the future agreed
- [ ] Shared accounts and family groups cleaned up
- [ ] Requests sent for work website, press releases and conference pages
- [ ] Requests sent for school, alumni and academic profile pages
- [ ] Requests sent for race results, federation, club and association pages
- [ ] Anonymisation requests sent to news sites and publishers
- [ ] Wikipedia and family tree sites checked
- [ ] Site administrators contacted about things written about me

## Real-world traces (7)

- [ ] ★ Saved addresses deleted from apps; old home and car listings removed
- [ ] ★ Blurring requested in Street View, Apple Look Around and Yandex for home, car and face
- [ ] ★ Location histories deleted; location sharing reviewed
- [ ] Open electoral register / directory listings opted out (where applicable)
- [ ] Fitness app activities hidden; privacy zones set
- [ ] Wi-Fi network name ends in `_nomap`
- [ ] Voice assistant recordings deleted; smart device and car app accounts cleaned up
- [ ] Health app and portal sharing settings reviewed
- [ ] (If applicable) DNA data deletion and sample destruction requested

## Legal rights (8)

- [ ] Deletion requests sent to every company without a delete option, or where I'm not sure
- [ ] Send dates and deadlines recorded
- [ ] Reminders sent where there was no reply
- [ ] Complaints filed with the data protection authority where needed

## Final steps (9)

- [ ] Decision made on the domain
- [ ] Contact details on services I'm keeping moved to the new email and number
- [ ] SMS two-factor authentication switched to an authenticator app
- [ ] Old emails and numbers closed after confirming nothing is tied to them
- [ ] Cloud storage emptied, trash cleared
- [ ] Devices removed from accounts and reset with encryption on
- [ ] Payment and crypto accounts closed
- [ ] Final decision made on the password manager

## Dead man's switch (10, optional)

- [ ] Google Inactive Account Manager and Telegram auto-deletion set up
- [ ] Scheduled emails or digital executor instructions prepared
- [ ] (If using the tool) switch set up, tested, armed, and check-in reminder in my calendar

## Living invisibly (11)

- [ ] ★ Official / everyday / anonymous identity layers separated
- [ ] ★ Using an email alias service
- [ ] ★ Phone and computer permissions reviewed
- [ ] Browser, search engine, DNS and (if needed) VPN set up
- [ ] Disappearing messages on in messaging apps
- [ ] Using virtual cards and pickup points
- [ ] ★ Sharing rules explained to the people close to me
- [ ] (Level 3) new number, new email, new devices; no accounts shared with my old circle

## Monitoring and maintenance (13)

- [ ] ★ Google Alerts and Have I Been Pwned notifications on
- [ ] Monthly: deletion confirmations and request reminders
- [ ] ★ Every three months: search engine, username and data broker checks
- [ ] Yearly: privacy settings, inventory, Street View, dead man's switch
